# Example Origin and Rights

These small synthetic resources were authored for the Design-DX convention draft.
They contain no company logos or copied third-party design kit. The bundled
convention diagram embeds Source Sans Pro font subsets under the included
[SIL Open Font License](convention/diagrams/OFL.txt), separately from the original
example material's MIT license.
The name Example Device does not describe a real product or its behavior.

These original example resources and the bundled Design-DX convention are covered by
the included [MIT License](LICENSE). Preserve its copyright and permission notices
when redistributing copies or substantial portions. When adapting this example,
record the origin and rights of any newly added material separately; it does not
automatically inherit Design-DX's license.

The included token uses the DTCG format, and the optional check uses Vale's rule
syntax. Neither DTCG nor Vale endorses the example. Their executables and source
are not bundled here.
